# Student Quick Start

CoolHack is an independent fictional cybersecurity simulation. Use only the
private team code provided by your professor.

## What you need

- Your private team code
- An invented screen name
- A new CoolHack-only password of at least 10 characters

Do not use your real name, institutional email, student ID, institutional
password, grade, phone number, real employer information, or real incident
data.

## Create access

1. Open the ordinary CoolHack student page.
2. Enter the private team code exactly as provided.
3. Choose an invented screen name.
4. Create a password used only for CoolHack.
5. Save the password privately; do not share it with teammates or the
   professor.
6. Select **Create student access**.

If the account already exists, enter the same team code, screen name, and
password, then select **Sign in**.

## Your team

Each team has four seats:

- SOC Analyst
- Incident Responder
- Security Lead
- Communications Lead

Your professor assigns one seat to each student. Everyone examines the same
evidence, but each role brings a different responsibility.

## Complete a mission

1. Read the scenario and decision point.
2. Inspect every artifact; do not click simulated suspicious links.
3. Write observations in **My role notes**.
4. Discuss evidence with the other three roles.
5. Record the team's initial conclusion before using AI.
6. One team member opens a permitted AI tool and shares the screen.
7. Answer the AI supervisor's questions as a team; do not ask it for the
   answer.
8. Paste the complete conversation into the shared AI transcript.
9. Complete one shared team report.
10. Write your own private reflection of at least 100 words.
11. Wait until the page confirms saving before leaving.

## Who can see what

- Teammates can see your invented screen name, assigned role, and role notes.
- Teammates can see and edit the shared report and AI transcript.
- Teammates cannot see your private reflection.
- Your assigned professor and the platform administrator can review the team's
  work and your reflection.

## Safety rules

- Use only fictional mission data.
- Never enter any real password in a mission, report, transcript, or AI chat.
- Never reuse an institutional or personal-account password for CoolHack.
- Do not share the team code outside your team.
- Do not try another team's code or another person's account.
- Report unexpected access or unexpected content to the professor.

## Common problems

- **Invalid team code:** check capitalization and ask the professor to confirm
  the code privately.
- **Account does not match:** use the same spelling, team code, and password
  used during account creation.
- **Role pending:** the professor still needs to assign your seat.
- **Workspace locked:** the professor has closed editing for that mission.
- **Save appears delayed:** stop typing, wait for the save message, and refresh
  only after it finishes.

Your professor should never ask for your CoolHack password.
